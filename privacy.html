Food Fight privacy

Grudge Match Games · last updated 23 November 2026

What the game keeps

  • On your device: your settings, your name, your scores, your cook level and your daily challenge streak.
  • Scores and levels: when you finish a game, the score, the name you play under, the mode and a random id for your device are sent to our server so the leaderboards and your cook level work.
  • If you sign in with a Grudge Match Games account: your email address and password (handled by our sign-in provider), your username and display name, your fighter picture, your friends, invites you send or receive, your weekly Grudge Rating and which device ids belong to your account.
  • Online matches: while you play online, your name, fighter and board are shared with the other players in your room. Emotes are fixed lines; there is no free-text chat.

What it doesn't do

  • No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, and nothing is sold.
  • No analytics service and no location.

Deleting your data

Signed in, open Friends and tap Delete my Food Fight data. That removes your Food Fight profile, friends, ratings, scores and cook level. Your Grudge Match Games login stays, because the other Grudge Match games use it; to delete the login itself, email us.
