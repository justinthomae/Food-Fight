How to play

  • 1Drag a tile left, right, up or down to swap it. Line up 3 of the same shape.
  • 2Big plays earn items for your rack, which holds 3. Tap one to throw it at the cook in the gold Aim box. Aim moves to the next cook every 3 seconds, so time your tap.
  • 3Pies thrown at you wait in the tray, then splat into your board in the same spot they were made on the thrower's board, pushing your tiles up. Anything you earn during the countdown blocks them first.
  • 4Break junk by matching next to it. Hold Raise for more tiles. Don't hit the top.
Game modes
  • 🧾Order Up! Same board, new goal. Tickets on the rail ask for foods ("4 tomatoes + 5 cheese"). Matches fill the oldest ticket first, and the first kitchen to fill 10 orders wins. Rush tickets 🔥 go cold if you're slow but pay a tip and a hot sauce. Topping out doesn't knock you out here: you spill, lose an order and freeze for a moment.
  • 🍩Snack Stack Pairs of food fall from the top. Drag sideways to slide, tap the board (or Spin) to turn, flick down or tap Drop to slam it. 4 or more of one food touching in any shape pops. When a pop makes the stack fall into another pop, that's a chain, and chains send big junk. Your pops cancel junk that's coming at you. Don't fill the ✕ column. Controls: by default, a slide pad sits under your left thumb (tap or slide it to move) with Spin above Drop under your right thumb. Prefer gestures? Switch to "Swipe the board" in Colors & look. Some pairs carry a gold-badge food: pop it to rack an item. Throw Steam (fog), Greasy fingers (hides their ghost and next pair), Hot sauce (faster fall), Brain freeze, Blender or Switcheroo at your target, or use a Rolling pin (flattens your top 3 rows) or Clean plate (clears your most common food) on yourself.
  • 🎯Splat Shot Food hangs from the ceiling. Slide your finger left or right anywhere on the board to turn your aim (a small slide is a small turn), then let go to throw. The dotted line, in the colour of your food, shows exactly where it'll stick, bounces included. Drag down before letting go to cancel. The food you're throwing and the next one sit in the bar under the board; tap Next or Swap to trade them. Hit 3 or more of one food to pop them, and anything left hanging drops too. Drops hit hardest. Junk you receive arrives as crust rows that push the ceiling down. Past the dashed line, you're out. Gold-badge foods hang in some rows: pop or drop one to rack its item, then tap it to throw. Steam fogs their food, Greasy fingers hides their aim guide and makes it wobble, Hot sauce drops their ceiling, plus Brain freeze, Blender and Switcheroo. Rainbow sprinkles make your next throw match anything it touches, and a Chili bomb blasts everything around where it lands, crust included.
  • 👑Boss Brawl Co-op. Everyone plays their own board, and every throw hits the giant boss. The boss announces its next attack 5 seconds ahead, then fires at one of you. Hold a rack item for half a second to pass it to the teammate in the most trouble. Beat the boss before it cleans out the whole kitchen.
How to earn attacks
Pie · Match 4 or more at once. Drops junk on them equal to tiles matched, minus one.
Pie tower · Make a chain: after your match clears, the tiles that fall make another match on their own. Every extra match in that chain adds a full row of junk, and the rows are thrown together as one tall stack. So your match plus 2 falling matches throws 2 rows of junk.
Hot sauce · Break junk. Their stack rises fast for 3 seconds, unless they keep clearing.
Food streaks

Clear the same food in back-to-back matches to build a streak. The badge on your board shows which food you're on.

  • ×2Double helping: that match scores double points.
  • ×3Snack attack: steal an item from the cook you're aiming at, and 2 of your junk blocks turn back into food.
  • ×4Feast: every tile of that food on your board clears at once. Then the streak starts over.
Special tiles

Some new rows bring up a tile with a gold badge. Clear it in any match to grab its item.

Whipped cream · Splats cream across their board. They have to rub it off with a finger before they can see or move the pieces underneath. It melts on its own after 15 seconds.
Brain freeze · They can't move tiles for 2½ seconds.
Blender · Scrambles the top 3 rows of their stack.
Rolling pin · Use it on yourself: flattens your top 3 rows. Great when you're near the top.
Switcheroo · Swaps your whole board with the cook you're aiming at. Your score, rack and orders stay yours, but their stack becomes your problem. Save it for when your stack is about to top out and theirs is low.
Clean plate · Use it on yourself: tap it, then tap any food on your board. Every tile of that food clears at once. Tap it again to cancel.

After 50 seconds, overtime makes every throw hit harder. Knocking a cook out powers up your throws by 25%.

Keyboard: arrows move, Space swaps sideways, C swaps down, Shift raises, 1–3 throws.
