Food Fight

Match food, chain clears, and splat pies on the other cooks. Last kitchen standing wins.

Practice vs bots

How to play

  • 1Drag a tile left, right, up or down to swap it. Line up 3 of the same shape.
  • 2Big plays earn items for your rack, which holds 3. Tap one to throw it at the cook in the gold Aim box. Aim moves to the next cook every 3 seconds, so time your tap.
  • 3Pies thrown at you wait in the tray. Anything you earn during the countdown blocks them first.
  • 4Break junk by matching next to it. Hold Raise for more tiles. Don't hit the top.
How to earn attacks
Pie · Match 4 or more at once. Drops junk on them equal to tiles matched, minus one.
Pie tower · Set off a chain. One full row of junk per chain step, thrown as a single tower.
Hot sauce · Break junk. Their stack rises fast for 3 seconds, unless they keep clearing.
Special tiles

Some new rows bring up a tile with a gold badge. Clear it in any match to grab its item.

Whipped cream · Covers their board in cream for 5 seconds.
Brain freeze · They can't move tiles for 2½ seconds.
Blender · Scrambles the top 3 rows of their stack.
Rolling pin · Use it on yourself: flattens your top 3 rows. Great when you're near the top.

After 50 seconds, overtime makes every throw hit harder. Knocking a cook out powers up your throws by 25%.

Keyboard: arrows move, Space swaps sideways, C swaps down, Shift raises, 1–3 throws.
